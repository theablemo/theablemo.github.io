## When should an AI system join a conversation?

InsightToast explores how relevant information can arrive at a useful moment without becoming another interruption. It brings together a multi-agent retrieval pipeline, peripheral interface design, and the study of how people use information in conversation.

## What connects the work

The system and the interface have to work together: deciding what information to retrieve is inseparable from deciding how and when people encounter it.
