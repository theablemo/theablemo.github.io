## How can AI support an expert without taking over?

AInsight listens to a conversation, identifies relevant problems and suggested solutions, and retrieves historical data to generate concise insights. Mohammad led development of the research prototype, connecting speech processing, retrieval, and an interface for expert use.

## What connects the work

The work connects systems engineering with a human-centered question: what helps a practitioner remain engaged with the person in front of them?
