Mohammad builds AI tools that help people make sense of complex information and make informed decisions.

At Waterloo, he explores [how AI can support decisions during conversations](/projects/insighttoast/). At Verily, he helps build healthcare AI agents whose answers can be [traced back to evidence](#experience).

His work brings research, engineering, and interface design together—from an early question to a [working system](#work), and an [understanding of how people use it](#publications).
